The problem we solve

Your history shouldn’t live in a drawer full of mystery cables.

Most families have years of memories spread across devices that no one wants to sort through. It often becomes urgent when a phone breaks, a hard drive fails, or someone passes away.

Stillframe Works gives those files a clear home. You receive an organized archive, a plain-language guide, and a practical backup plan.

Questions people ask first

Before you hand over a single device.

Do I need to organize everything first?

No. A box of mixed devices is normal. We begin with an inventory and help decide what is worth including.

Can you work with a deceased family member’s devices?

Sometimes, when you have clear legal authority and the devices are accessible. We verify authorization before accepting estate-related work.

Can you recover a broken hard drive?

Stillframe Works is an organization and preservation service, not a clean-room recovery lab. If specialized recovery is needed, we can discuss a qualified partner and costs before anything leaves our care.

Do you keep a copy of my archive?

Only for the limited project period agreed in writing. Your final agreement will explain retention and deletion timing clearly.
